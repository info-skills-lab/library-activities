The research question

Your research question

Did Gemeni Collective infringe copyright in the Sun in Bloom artworks by relying on and reproducing substantial parts of Sunshine Designs' original artistic works when creating its competing garment collection?

Sunshine Designs Pty Ltd is an independent Australian fashion label founded by textile artist Sun Velasco, who creates original hand-drawn artworks that are incorporated into fabric prints used on garments, accessories and homewares. The artworks are developed through a lengthy creative process involving sketching, digital refinement, colour development and fabric sampling.

Between 2022 and 2024, Sunshine Designs released a highly successful collection featuring a distinctive floral and geometric print known as the "Sun in Bloom" series. The garments were widely promoted through social media, fashion magazines, runway events and retail stores across Australia.

In early 2025, Sunshine Designs discovered that Gemeni Collective Retail Group, a national fast-fashion retailer, had launched a new clothing range containing fabric prints that appeared strikingly similar to the Sun in Bloom artworks. The new collection included dresses, skirts, shirts and scarves displaying nearly identical floral arrangements, colour palettes, motifs and compositional elements.
