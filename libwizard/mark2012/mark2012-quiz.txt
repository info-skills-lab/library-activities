MARK2012 Check your learning: LibWizard quiz
============================================

How it's set up
- Add these to slide 12 in LibWizard, in this order, with a Page Break between each.
- Multiple choice questions: Radios. In Answer Properties, set the correct answer (✓) and tick Display correct/wrong messages.
- To make every question compulsory, tick Required on each question. Students can't continue until they answer it.
- Leave Require correct answer to continue unticked, so a wrong answer doesn't stop them.
- Settings: answers table on, retake off.
- The quiz end points students to Finish.
- Each question is tagged with its Digital Information Skills Framework area and source slide for your reference. Don't add the tags to LibWizard.


QUESTIONS (10)
--------------

1. Which words are the key concepts in "What are the key elements of a marketing strategy and a marketing plan, and how do the two differ?"
   a) marketing strategy, marketing plan, elements ✓
   b) what, how, differ
   c) key, two, the
   d) marketing only
   Correct: Correct. These are the ideas you search for. Words like "what" and "how" guide your answer but aren't searched.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. Key concepts are the main ideas you search for. Words like "what" and "how" aren't searched.
   [Identify, Slide 4]

2. Which search will find the most results?
   a) "marketing plan" AND "marketing strategy" AND element*
   b) "marketing plan" OR "marketing planning" ✓
   c) "marketing plan" AND "marketing planning"
   d) "key elements of a marketing plan and strategy"
   Correct: Correct. OR joins words for the same idea, so it finds results with either phrase and broadens your search.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. AND narrows a search and a long phrase in quotation marks narrows it further. OR broadens it.
   [Identify, Slide 4]

3. What does the asterisk do in the search word element*?
   a) Finds different word endings, such as element and elements ✓
   b) Searches for the exact word only
   c) Removes the word from your search
   d) Limits your results to recent years
   Correct: Correct. This is called truncation. It finds every ending of the word.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. The asterisk finds different word endings. This is called truncation.
   [Identify, Slide 4]

4. Where on the Library website do you find services that support your studies?
   a) Under Services in the top menu ✓
   b) Under Contact us
   c) In your Research log
   d) Under What's on
   Correct: Correct. Services lists study support such as student workshops and Library Peer Mentors.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. Look under Services in the top menu of the Library website.
   [Discover, Slide 2]

5. In the Library collection, which filter helps you find more current results?
   a) Dates ✓
   b) Topics
   c) Resource types
   d) Research Assistant
   Correct: Correct. Dates limits your results to the years you choose.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. Dates limits your results to the years you choose.
   [Discover, Slide 3]

6. Which database is the best place to start for a report on an Australian industry?
   a) IBISWorld ✓
   b) Library collection
   c) A general search engine
   d) Research Assistant
   Correct: Correct. IBISWorld publishes industry reports covering performance, products and markets, competition and major companies.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. IBISWorld publishes industry reports for Australia. You'll find it in the Business subject guide.
   [Discover, Slides 5 and 6]

7. You search IBISWorld for soy milk. Which report covers it?
   a) Soy and Almond Milk Production in Australia ✓
   b) Milk and Cream Processing in Australia
   c) Dairy Cattle Farming in Australia
   d) There is no report, as nothing is titled Soy Milk
   Correct: Correct. Industries aren't always named the way you search for them, so read the titles before you choose.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. IBISWorld groups soy, almond and other plant-based milks in one report, Soy and Almond Milk Production in Australia.
   [Discover, Slide 6]

8. What does peer reviewed mean?
   a) Checked by experts in the field before publication ✓
   b) Written by a university student
   c) Published within the last five years
   d) Available in full text
   Correct: Correct. Peer review is a quality check by experts, which makes these articles a credible source.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. Peer-reviewed articles are checked by experts in the field before publication.
   [Evaluate, Slide 7]

9. A report on your industry was published 12 years ago. Which part of the CRAAP test does it fail?
   a) Currency ✓
   b) Authority
   c) Purpose
   d) Accuracy
   Correct: Correct. Industries change quickly. Look for the most recent report.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. The problem is the date. Currency asks whether a source is up to date for your topic.
   [Evaluate, Slide 9]

10. An AI tool gives you a statistic with no source. What should you do?
   a) Find and check the original source before you use it ✓
   b) Use it, as AI tools are always accurate
   c) Ask the AI tool to repeat the statistic
   d) Reword the statistic so it doesn't need a source
   Correct: Correct. AI can produce figures that are incorrect or made up. Check them against a trusted source, such as a Library database.
   Wrong: That’s incorrect. AI can produce figures that are incorrect or made up, so find and check the original source first.
   [Use responsibly, Slide 10]
