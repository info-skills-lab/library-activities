MARK2051 Check your learning: LibWizard quiz
============================================

How it's set up
- Add these to slide 10 in LibWizard, in this order, with a Page Break between each.
- Multiple choice questions: Radios. On Answer Properties, set the answer (✓), tick Display correct/wrong messages, and leave Require correct answer to continue unticked.
- Confidence ratings: Rating field, Radio button type, Horizontal, not required, Answer set to Not Selected. Scale 1 Not at all confident, 5 Very confident.
- Settings: answers table on, retake off.
- The quiz end points students to Finish.
- The source slide is noted after each question for your reference. Don't add the notes to LibWizard.


PART A. BEFORE YOU START (rating scale, 1 to 5)
-----------------------------------------------
Prompt: How confident are you that you can do the following?

A1. Turn a question into keywords and a database search.
A2. Find peer-reviewed research and consumer data in Library databases.
A3. Evaluate a source using the CRAAP test.
A4. Use AI tools responsibly for your assessment.


PART B. QUESTIONS
-----------------

1. Which search finds articles that mention both trust and Copilot?
   a) trust AND Copilot ✓
   b) trust OR Copilot
   c) trust NOT Copilot
   Correct: Right. AND means every result includes both words.
   Wrong: Not quite. AND means every result includes both words.
   [Slide 2]

2. What does adopt* find?
   a) adopt, adoption, adopting and more ✓
   b) Only the word adopt
   c) Words that mean the same as adopt
   Correct: Right. The asterisk finds different word endings. This is called truncation.
   Wrong: Not quite. The asterisk finds different word endings, such as adoption and adopting.
   [Slide 2]

3. You need peer-reviewed research on consumer trust in AI. Where's a great place to start?
   a) Business Source Premier ✓
   b) A social media search
   c) A company's own website
   Correct: Right. Business Source Premier lets you limit to peer-reviewed articles.
   Wrong: Not quite. Business Source Premier is a scholarly database where you can limit to peer-reviewed articles.
   [Slide 3]

4. Which Scopus subject area helps most with understanding consumer motives?
   a) Psychology ✓
   b) Engineering
   c) Computer Science
   Correct: Right. Psychology studies how people think, feel and decide.
   Wrong: Not quite. Psychology studies how people think, feel and decide.
   [Slide 4]

5. Where can you find survey data on how different consumer segments feel about technology?
   a) Passport GMID ✓
   b) A textbook
   c) MarketLine company profiles
   Correct: Right. Passport has consumer surveys you can compare by segment.
   Wrong: Not quite. Passport GMID has consumer surveys you can compare by segment.
   [Slide 5]

6. Which of these is grey literature?
   a) A government report on AI use in Australia ✓
   b) A peer-reviewed journal article
   c) A textbook from a commercial publisher
   Correct: Right. Grey literature is published outside commercial and academic publishers.
   Wrong: Not quite. Grey literature is published outside commercial and academic publishers, such as government reports.
   [Slide 6]

7. You find a 2015 report on attitudes to AI. Which part of the CRAAP test does it fail?
   a) Currency ✓
   b) Relevance
   c) Accuracy
   Correct: Right. Attitudes to AI have changed a lot. Look for recent data.
   Wrong: Not quite. Check the date. Attitudes to AI have changed a lot since then.
   [Slide 8]

8. An AI tool gives you a reference. What should you do first?
   a) Search for it in a Library database to check it exists ✓
   b) Add it to your reference list
   c) Ask the AI if it's real
   Correct: Right. AI tools can make up references that look real.
   Wrong: Not quite. AI tools can make up references, so always check each one yourself.
   [Slide 9]
