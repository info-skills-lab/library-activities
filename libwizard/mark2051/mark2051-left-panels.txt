========== 2. Breaking down the question ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Breaking down the question</h3><p>Every research question has key parts. Breaking them down helps you understand the question, and turns it into keywords you can search in databases, search engines and AI tools.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Spin each wheel and select <strong>Add</strong> to pick your words.</li><li>Add more than one word on a wheel to include synonyms.</li><li>Select <strong>+ Add a wheel</strong> for your own idea.</li></ol><p>Your search is saved and used on the next slides.</p><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the search lock activity in a new window</a></p>

========== 3. Business Source Premier ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Business Source Premier</h3><p>Business Source Premier is a scholarly business database covering most areas of business research. Alongside peer-reviewed articles, it holds company and industry reports, conference papers, news and much more.</p><p>It’s a great place to start for this assessment.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Set the practice filters on the right and check them.</li><li>Then try your search for real.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Business Source Premier activity in a new window</a></p>

========== 4. Scopus ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Scopus</h3><p>Scopus is a multidisciplinary database covering many subjects, including business and psychology.</p><p>Psychology research helps you understand the motives of your consumers, and why they may or may not be willing to use AI in their everyday lives.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Pick the subject areas that fit best.</li><li>Then try your search for real.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=3" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Scopus activity in a new window</a></p>

========== 5. Passport GMID ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Passport GMID</h3><p>Passport GMID is a Library database of country, industry and consumer data. It includes survey data on different consumer segments and what drives them, across a wide range of topics.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Read the practice chart on the right.</li><li>Answer both questions.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=4" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Passport GMID activity in a new window</a></p>

========== 6. Grey literature ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Grey literature</h3><p>Grey literature is research published outside commercial and academic publishers. Think government reports, think tank papers, industry surveys and conference papers.</p><p>It’s often current and practical, which makes it handy for a fast-moving topic like AI.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Decide whether each source is grey literature.</li><li>Build a Google search to find some.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=5" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the grey literature activity in a new window</a></p>

========== 7. MarketLine ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">MarketLine</h3><p>MarketLine is a global company and industry research database. Its company profiles show who a company is, what it does and how well it’s performing in its industry. It also indexes news.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Read each question on the right.</li><li>Select the profile section where you’d find the answer.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=6" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the MarketLine activity in a new window</a></p>

========== 8. Evaluating sources ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Evaluating sources</h3><p>Information quality varies. Before you use a source, check that it is</p><ul><li><strong>Relevant</strong> to your question and segment</li><li><strong>Current</strong>, especially for a topic like AI</li><li><strong>Reliable</strong>, from a source you can trace</li><li><strong>Accurate</strong>, with facts that check out</li><li><strong>Balanced</strong>, not biased by who wrote it</li></ul><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Pick the check each source fails.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=7" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the evaluating sources activity in a new window</a></p>
