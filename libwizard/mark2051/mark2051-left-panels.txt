========== 2. Breaking down the question ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Breaking down the question</h3><p>Every research question has key parts. Breaking them down helps you understand the question and turns it into keywords you can search in databases, search engines and AI tools.</p><p>Words for the same idea join with OR. Different ideas join with AND, so every result includes them all.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Spin each dial on the right and select <strong>Add</strong> to choose your words.</li><li>Add more than one word on a dial to include synonyms.</li><li>Select <strong>+ Add a dial</strong> for your own idea.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=2" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the search lock activity in a new window</a></p><div style="background:#d9edf7; border:1px solid #bce8f1; border-radius:4px; color:#1f4e66; margin:10px 0 0 0; padding:10px 14px"><p style="margin:0"><strong>Tip:</strong> An asterisk finds different word endings, so adopt* finds adopt, adoption and adopting. This is called truncation. Quotation marks keep words together as a phrase, such as "generative AI".</p></div><div style="background:#f5f5f5; border:1px solid #e3e3e3; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0 0 0; padding:10px 14px; font-size:0.9em"><p style="margin:0"><strong>Source:</strong> UNSW Library, <a href="https://subjectguides.library.unsw.edu.au/search-skills/question" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Search skills, breaking down your question</a>.</p></div>

========== 3. Business Source Premier ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Business Source Premier</h3><p>Business Source Premier is a scholarly business database covering most areas of business research. Alongside peer-reviewed articles, it holds company and industry reports, conference papers, news and much more. It’s a great place to start for this assessment.</p><p>Consider using it to find research on</p><ul><li>why consumers trust, or distrust, new technology</li><li>consumer attitudes to AI tools and virtual assistants</li><li>what drives consumers to adopt a new product or service</li><li>how brands build credibility with different consumer segments</li></ul><p>Peer-reviewed articles have been checked by other experts in the field before publication.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Check your search from the last slide in the rows on the right.</li><li>Set the filters, then select <strong>Search</strong>.</li><li>Select <strong>Run it in Business Source Premier</strong> and set the same filters on the real results page.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=3" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Business Source Premier activity in a new window</a></p>

========== 4. Scopus ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Scopus</h3><p>Scopus is a multidisciplinary database covering many subjects, including business and psychology. Psychology research helps you understand the motives of your consumers, and why they may or may not be willing to use AI in their everyday lives.</p><p>Like Business Source Premier, Scopus is made up largely of peer-reviewed research, which makes it a strong source of evidence for your arguments.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Pick the two subject areas that fit best, then select <strong>Search</strong>.</li><li>Select <strong>Run it in Scopus</strong> to see your results. Your subject areas carry across.</li></ol><p>Your search saves to your Research log automatically.</p><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=4" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Scopus activity in a new window</a></p><div style="background:#d9edf7; border:1px solid #bce8f1; border-radius:4px; color:#1f4e66; margin:10px 0 0 0; padding:10px 14px"><p style="margin:0"><strong>Tip:</strong> Save your search in Scopus and set an alert to get new results by email.</p></div>

========== 5. Passport GMID ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Passport GMID</h3><p>Passport GMID is a Library database of country, industry and consumer data. It’s especially valuable for real-world survey data on specific markets, showing how consumers behave across a range of topics, including technology and AI.</p><p>Survey data like this measures what consumers do. Research in Business Source Premier and Scopus helps explain why.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Read the practice data on the right.</li><li>Answer the questions in <strong>Read the data</strong> and <strong>Judge the survey</strong>.</li><li>Select <strong>Open Passport</strong> and follow the route to find the real data for your segment.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=5" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the Passport GMID activity in a new window</a></p><div style="background:#d9edf7; border:1px solid #bce8f1; border-radius:4px; color:#1f4e66; margin:10px 0 0 0; padding:10px 14px"><p style="margin:0"><strong>Tip:</strong> Data on AI use is spread across Passport. Create consumer profiles is the best place to start.</p></div>

========== 6. Grey literature ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Grey literature</h3><p>Grey literature is research published outside commercial and academic publishers. Think government reports, think tank papers, industry surveys and conference papers. It’s often current and practical, which makes it handy for a fast-moving topic like AI.</p><p>Websites ending in .gov.au belong to Australian governments, and .edu.au to Australian universities.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Decide whether each source is grey literature.</li><li>Build a Google search with the options below, then select <strong>Search Google</strong>.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=6" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the grey literature activity in a new window</a></p><div style="background:#d9edf7; border:1px solid #bce8f1; border-radius:4px; color:#1f4e66; margin:10px 0 0 0; padding:10px 14px"><p style="margin:0"><strong>Check it.</strong> Before you use any source, ask whether it’s relevant, current, reliable and accurate.</p></div>

========== 7. MarketLine ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">MarketLine</h3><p>MarketLine is a global company and industry research database. Its company profiles show who a company is, what it does and how well it’s performing in its industry.</p><p>A profile brings together an overview with key facts, a SWOT analysis, financials, news, a list of competitors and related research reports. The SWOT analysis is especially useful for understanding the wider environment a company operates in.</p><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Read the research need in the yellow card on the right.</li><li>Select the profile section that meets it, then select <strong>Next question</strong>.</li><li>Select <strong>Open MarketLine</strong> to look up Microsoft for real.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=7" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the MarketLine activity in a new window</a></p><div style="background:#d9edf7; border:1px solid #bce8f1; border-radius:4px; color:#1f4e66; margin:10px 0 0 0; padding:10px 14px"><p style="margin:0"><strong>Check it.</strong> Before you use any source, ask whether it’s relevant, current, reliable and accurate.</p></div>

========== 8. Evaluating sources ==========

<h3 style="border-bottom:4px solid #ffe000; padding-bottom:6px">Evaluating sources</h3><p>Information quality varies, and some sources contain bias or misinformation. Before you use a source, check that it is</p><ul><li><strong>Relevant</strong> to your question and segment</li><li><strong>Current</strong>, especially for a topic like AI</li><li><strong>Reliable</strong>, from a source you can trace, without bias</li><li><strong>Accurate</strong>, with facts that check out</li></ul><h4>What to do</h4><ol><li>Read each source on the right.</li><li>Pick the check it fails.</li></ol><p><a href="https://info-skills-lab.github.io/library-activities/consumer-research.html?part=8" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the evaluating sources activity in a new window</a></p>
